# Code of Conduct

Rajcord welcomes contributors and users of different backgrounds and experience levels.

- Be respectful and constructive, including during disagreements and code reviews.
- Do not engage in harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory abuse.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent without dismissing concerns.
- Do not publish anyone else's private information.
- Keep discussions on topic and use issues, discussions, and pull requests appropriately.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content, close disruptive discussions, and restrict participation when necessary. If GitHub content violates its policies, use GitHub's reporting tools. Do not post personal or sensitive reports publicly.
